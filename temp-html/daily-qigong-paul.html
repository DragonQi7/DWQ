01

Spirit Gate breathing

A quiet bedtime practice. Bring your attention to your belly button.

☾

Before bed · about 2–3 minutes, or less

In: belly in.
Out: belly out.

  1. Get comfortable. Sit with support. Relax your shoulders and jaw.
  2. Breathe in through your nose. Gently draw your belly button inward.
  3. Breathe out slowly through your mouth. Let your belly soften outward.
  4. Repeat without strain. Finish by letting your breathing return to normal.

This is a gentle version of the source’s reverse breathing. No breath holds here. Keep the movement small; stop if you feel dizzy or uncomfortable.

1. InhaleNavel draws inward
2. ExhaleBelly softens outward

Watch the direction. Use your own comfortable breathing pace.

The four energy gates

Hand → chest → lower belly → buttocks. These are the four gates taught in your TCMWORLD notes.

Keep it easy. For a short introduction, try 30–60 seconds per area, doing both hands and both buttocks. This is a shortened starter suggestion; the source’s full counts are below.
Gentle contact, relaxed breathing. The dots show approximate areas. Learn exact placement from your teacher. Skip any sore, injured, or irritated area.
GATE 1 · HAND

He Gu

Small thumb circles

LI 4
Back of hand

Find it: The fleshy web area between your thumb and index finger.

Support your left hand with your right. Use your right thumb to make small, comfortable circles. Then switch hands.

Small circles · both hands

If pregnant, skip pressure on LI 4 unless your maternity clinician says it is appropriate.

GATE 2 · CHEST

Tan Zhong

Soft circles over the center

CV 17
Front view

Find it: The center line of your chest, on the breastbone between the breasts.

Rest one palm here and place the other on top. Make soft, small circles one way, then the other. Use light pressure only.

Overlapping palms · light touch
GATE 3 · LOWER BELLY

Guan Yuan

Alternate gentle fingertip taps

CV 4
Front view

Find it: The lower-belly center line. Your book describes the area as about five finger-widths below the navel.

Use your index and middle fingers together. Tap lightly with one hand, then the other. Keep your belly relaxed.

Alternate two fingertips · no hard poking

This is below the belly button; Spirit Gate breathing focuses on the belly button itself. If pregnant, skip abdominal tapping.

GATE 4 · BUTTOCKS

Huan Tiao

Loose fists, easy side-to-side sway

GB 30
Back view

Find it: The fleshy buttock area on each side, around the muscle’s dimple—not your spine or waist.

Stand comfortably with soft knees. Make loose fists and gently tap one side, then the other. Let a small side-to-side sway help your arms move.

Loose fists · gentle taps · easy sway
03

Let the body rest.

You do not have to keep doing, fixing, or pushing all day.

Notice the feeling.
Soften the effort.

In the TCM teaching, prolonged worry and emotional struggle use Qi. In everyday terms, staying tense or replaying a problem can feel exhausting.

Feelings are natural. You do not need to force them away or blame yourself for having them.

1. Notice

“I’m feeling worried,” or “I’m getting frustrated.”

2. Soften

Unclench your jaw. Drop your shoulders. Take an easy breath.

3. Pause

Put the phone down. Sit quietly. Leave the next task for a moment.

Rest is part of your practice, too.
